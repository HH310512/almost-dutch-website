What is Almost Dutch?

A playful app that teaches you the unwritten rules of Dutch culture through 150 real-life scenarios — the birthday circle, Dutch directness, the agenda cult. Made for expats and internationals in the Netherlands.

Is it free?

Three full worlds (45 scenarios) are free forever — no account, no ads, no tricks. The other seven worlds unlock with one payment of €11.99 in the App Store and Google Play.

What about my data?

Privacy-first, genuinely: I only ask for an email address, there's no third-party tracking, and removing your data takes one message. From launch, all account data will be stored in the EU. That's the whole policy, more or less.

When does it launch?

Autumn 2026, in the App Store and Google Play. The waitlist gets one email on the day it's there. It's at the top of my agenda, and you know how seriously agendas are taken here.
